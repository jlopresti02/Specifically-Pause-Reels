# Reyes vs Jones pause game: trial reel test

## What past pause game trials showed (@wwitmma owner insights, Oct 28 2026)

| Game | Length | Variants | Plays per variant | Avg watched | Skip rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Merab vs Yan cage drop (Oct 20) | 6s | ~10 | 50 to 316 (best: 316, no caption) | 45% to 108% | 63% to 86% |
| Volk gets his hair back (Oct 21) | 9s | ~14 | 52 to 350 (most 60 to 95) | 49% to 80% | 64% to 80% |
| Elmo and Evloev numbers (Oct 22) | 8s | ~12 | 0 to 69 (half got 0 plays) | 39% to 65% | 62% to 76% |

What this tells us:
- **Short loops win.** Only the 6s Merab reel cleared 100% average watched, which means people rewatched it. All Reyes versions stay 6.0s with the hamster dance loop.
- **A "can you" hook did best.** The Merab reel's on-screen hook was "CAN YOU TRAP MERAB?", so version D tests "CAN YOU STOP".
- **Captions didn't seem to matter much.** The top Merab reel had no caption, and the captioned copies did worse. All six Reyes versions share one caption, so the on-screen change is the only difference.
- **Posting many copies at once splits the reach.** The Elmo batch had 12 copies posted within 8 minutes, and half got 0 plays. This test uses 6 copies spaced 10 minutes apart.
